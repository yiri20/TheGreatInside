## Operational notes

Research ran as agent batches of one to three candidates, never more than two
agents at once. Usage, not time, was the binding constraint: one agent costs
roughly 15-20% of a 5-hour window per two to three candidates, and the weekly
window rose from 48% to about 86% over this cycle (the cost cap cut per-candidate
cost by about a third from Roster50). Cycle wall-clock time was dominated by
waiting for 5-hour windows to reset (monitors on an epoch time). No agent was cut
off mid-candidate this cycle. Gödel and Mirzakhani were the only blockers and
both are rights questions that need a human to write to an archive.
